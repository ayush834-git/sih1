### Overall Audit Verdict
“The implementation and evaluation are technically defensible against expert scrutiny, subject to the documented limitations and partial coverage of unseen-family generalization and enterprise/CII validation.”

### Zero-Overclaim Commitments
1. **Model Nature**: Described strictly as an "interpretable autoregressive temporal world-model formulation for discrete network states", NOT a deep generative model, Transformer, or GNN.
2. **Risk vs Probability**: Heuristic Future Security Risk $R(t+h)$ is strictly designated as a normalized priority index $[0, 1]$, never an attack probability. Calibrated Progression Probability is an independent, Platt-calibrated lookahead classifier ($P(\text{Attack} \mid \text{history}) \in [0, 1]$).
3. **Benchmark Realism**: We report that AR(5) achieves higher recall (+20.5% over static LR) at the expense of false alarms (66.6% FPR), while calibrated progression achieves 86.4% precision with 0.60% FPR. We never claim universal superiority.
4. **Lead Time Reality**: A 10-second prediction horizon does not guarantee 10 seconds of human SOC reaction time in noisy enterprise networks.
